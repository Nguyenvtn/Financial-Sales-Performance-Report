# Business Insights from Financial & Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes a **financial and sales performance dataset** for a fictional manufacturing company selling products across multiple countries and customer segments.

Using **Power BI**, the goal was to build an interactive dashboard that provides meaningful business insights into sales performance, profitability, cost structure, product performance, and forecast accuracy.

---

## Key Business Questions

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

* How is the company performing in terms of sales, cost, and profit?
* Which countries contribute the most to net sales?
* Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
* How do actual sales compare with forecast sales?
* Which products are overperforming or underperforming against forecast?
* How is profit margin changing over time?

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales Performance Is Strong, but Cost Remains High

The company generated **$99.23M in net sales**, with **COGS of $84.97M** and **net profit of $14.26M**.

**Key observations**

* The company achieved almost **$100M** in net sales.
* COGS accounts for a large share of total sales.
* The overall profit margin is **14.4%**.

**Business implication**

Management should focus on cost control, pricing strategy, and product mix optimization to improve profitability.

---

### 2. Revenue Is Mainly Driven by Government and Small Business Segments

Segment analysis shows that **Government** and **Small Business** are the strongest revenue contributors.

**Key observations**

* Government generated around **$45M** in sales.
* Small Business generated around **$34M** in sales.
* Enterprise, Midmarket, and Channel Partners contribute much less revenue.

**Business implication**

The company should protect its strong customer segments while exploring growth opportunities in weaker segments such as Midmarket and Channel Partners.

---

### 3. Sales Are Well Distributed Across Countries

Country analysis shows that sales are relatively balanced across Canada, Germany, the United States, France, and Mexico.

**Key observations**

* Canada, Germany, the United States, France, and Mexico each contribute a meaningful share of total sales.
* No single country completely dominates revenue.
* This creates a diversified geographic sales base.

**Business implication**

A balanced country portfolio reduces dependency risk and gives the company more stable international sales performance.

---

### 4. Actual Sales Are Slightly Below Forecast

Forecast analysis shows that actual sales are below the sales target.

**Key observations**

* Actual Sales reached **$99.23M**.
* Forecast Sales were **$102.6M**.
* The company achieved **96.72%** of the forecast target.
* The total gap to target is around **3.28%**.

**Business implication**

The company should review forecast assumptions and identify why several months and products are underperforming against plan.

---

### 5. Product Performance Varies Across the Portfolio

Product analysis shows clear differences between products.

**Key observations**

* Paseo generated the highest actual sales.
* VTT slightly exceeded forecast expectations.
* Montana, Amarilla, and Carretera underperformed against forecast.
* Most products show negative forecast variance.

**Business implication**

Management should investigate why VTT outperformed and apply similar sales, pricing, or promotion strategies to weaker products.

---

### 6. Profit Margin Changes Significantly Over Time

Monthly profit margin analysis shows visible fluctuations throughout the reporting period.

**Key observations**

* Profit margin reached higher levels in some months.
* March 2024 shows a strong margin decline.
* Margin volatility may be caused by discounts, product mix, or cost changes.

**Business implication**

The company should monitor margin movement regularly and investigate months with low profitability to protect overall business performance.

---

## Conclusion

This Power BI analysis demonstrates how financial and sales data can be transformed into an executive dashboard for business decision-making.

Key takeaways:

* The company generated strong net sales of **$99.23M**.
* COGS remains high and puts pressure on profitability.
* Government and Small Business are the main revenue drivers.
* Sales are well distributed across countries.
* Actual sales are slightly below forecast.
* Product-level performance shows both opportunities and risks.
* Profit margin requires continuous monitoring.

These insights highlight the value of **Power BI-based financial and sales analysis in monitoring business performance, improving forecast accuracy, and supporting strategic decisions**.
